Basal Soil Preparation

Since soil Nitrogen is in the high optimal range (92 kg/ha), avoid excessive chemical Urea application during the first 25 days to prevent stem elongation and lodging.

Stage: Pre-Transplant Priority: High
🌧️

Monsoon Drainage Strategy

In heavy clay and black cotton soils, dig 30 cm deep perimeter bund trenches to safely carry away excess runoff during abrupt 40mm+ cloudburst days.

Stage: Week 2–4 Priority: Medium
🐛

Bio-Pest Alert: Yellow Stem Borer

Warm day temperatures (26–28°C) with elevated humidity promote early stem borer moths in paddy nurseries. Erect 5 pheromone traps per acre.

Stage: Nursery Seedbed Priority: Immediate
